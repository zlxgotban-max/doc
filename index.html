Built for real products

Explore how FlexFold PCB fits into different categories of compact hardware.

Invisible electronics, visible comfort

FlexFold PCB bends around wrists, collars and straps so your electronics disappear into the fabric of everyday life without sacrificing durability.

  • Route sensors across curved surfaces without rigid boards.
  • Keep weight and profile low for all‑day comfort.
  • Design housings around people instead of rectangles.

Freedom for joints and grippers

Run power, feedback and control lines directly through moving joints while protecting traces from repeated flexing.

  • Eliminate cable chains in tight robotic arms.
  • Anchor components on rigid islands and flex between them.
  • Reduce failure points compared to discrete harnesses.

Cleaner internals for compact devices

Simplify assembly in headphones, controllers and handhelds by turning cables, connectors and PCBs into a single flexible spine.

  • Shrink housing depth while keeping ports and buttons aligned.
  • Improve serviceability with predictable routing paths.
  • Deliver a premium feel through solid mechanical design.

Questions, meet answers

A quick overview of what to expect when you use FlexFold PCB in your next product.

Flex behavior
How flexible is FlexFold PCB?
FlexFold PCB is built to bend around compact radii and survive repeated motion, assuming you respect recommended bend zones in your enclosure. Treat it like a living hinge for your signals rather than a rigid plate.
Integration
Can I adapt it to my layout?
Yes. We start from this reference board and adapt trace routing, island locations, and connector strategy to your mechanical model, keeping all flex‑safe rules in place.
Manufacturing
Do you also manufacture the PCB?
FlexFold PCB is offered as a design‑ready reference. You remain free to choose your manufacturer, and we provide a stack‑up and design package that they can accept with minimal adjustments.
Support
What about design support?
With each unit you can request a short design consult focused on bend radii, component placement on islands, and routing strategies between rigid and flexible zones.
