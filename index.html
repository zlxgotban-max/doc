What is Q‑Mesh Aura?
🌐

A new way to think about connectivity

Today, networks belong to places — your home router, your office Wi-Fi, a hotel's connection. Q‑Mesh Aura flips this entirely. It is a small, wearable node that generates a private network bubble around your body, anchoring all your devices — laptop, phone, AR glasses, smartwatch, even your car — into a single seamless mesh that moves with you.

Instead of reconnecting every time you switch environments, your bubble silently negotiates with whatever infrastructure is nearby — a 6G cell tower, a LEO satellite, a public fiber point — renting just the bandwidth you need for as long as you need it. No passwords, no handoffs, no dropped calls when you step outside.

⚙️

How the hardware works

The Q‑Mesh Aura node is a compact device — roughly the size of a thick coin — that clips onto clothing or fits in a pocket. Inside: a multi-band radio module (6G, Wi-Fi 8, UWB), a dedicated quantum-safe encryption chip, and a mesh coordination processor. It runs for up to 72 hours on a single charge and auto-updates its firmware silently overnight.
